From passages to coordinated views

The 2,134 passages were encoded using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. KMeans grouped the original 384-dimensional embeddings into eight clusters, which were labeled using common TF-IDF terms and representative passages. UMAP then projected the embeddings into 2D for visualization. The x- and y-axes are left unlabeled because they do not represent specific semantic variables.

On the map, position shows semantic similarity, color shows topic, and circle size shows passage length. Search and filters work together, while clicking a passage highlights its section–topic matrix cell and shows five similar passages based on cosine similarity. Clicking a matrix cell also highlights matching passages on the map. Together, the two views connect semantic similarity with the bulletin’s original structure.
