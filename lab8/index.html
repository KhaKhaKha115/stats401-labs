Duke Kunshan University Undergraduate Instruction Bulletin

This project examines the Duke Kunshan University Undergraduate Instruction Bulletin, 2021–2022, a 400-page record of the university’s curriculum, programs, policies, courses, and student services. One paragraph or short policy block is treated as one passage, while chapter, section, subsection, page, and original text are preserved.

06 · DESIGN DESCRIPTION

From passages to coordinated views

The 2,134 prepared passages were encoded with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Sentence Transformer, with embedding normalization enabled. KMeans grouped the original 384-dimensional vectors—not the display coordinates—into eight clusters using random_state=401 and n_init="auto". Cluster labels were assigned only after inspecting characteristic TF-IDF terms and the five passages nearest each cluster centroid. UMAP then projected the embeddings to two dimensions with 15 neighbors, minimum distance 0.15, cosine distance, and random seed 401. The projection supports neighborhood exploration, but its x and y directions are intentionally unlabeled because they are not independent semantic variables.

On the map, position represents the UMAP projection, hue represents topic, and circle area represents passage word count. Low opacity keeps dense regions legible; hover and persistent selection provide exact context. Search, section, topic, and matrix-cell constraints are combined in one shared state so one interaction cannot erase another. Selecting a passage highlights its section–topic matrix cell and retrieves five neighbors precomputed with cosine similarity in the original embedding space. Conversely, selecting a matrix cell highlights its matching map passages. The matrix uses color intensity for count and reports within-section proportions in its tooltip. Together, the views let readers move between local semantic similarity and the bulletin’s authored hierarchy while retaining the original passage text for interpretation.
