TEMPORAL NETWORK QUESTIONS

What changes over time?

QuestionAnswer
01How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days? The first 15 days average 5.4 links and 8.5 active companies per day. In the final 15 days, those rise to 7.1 links and 9.5 active companies. This indicates that the network becomes denser and includes more active companies over time.
02Which companies become more central or commercially active over time? Fusion Electronics has the largest cumulative activity at about $1.99M, followed by Ion Systems at about $1.49M and Cedar Retail at about $1.29M. Their frequent and high-value transactions make them the most commercially active companies in the network.
03Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters? Yes. Days 1, 7, 11, 13, 17, and 19 contain only three links among six active companies. During these periods, the network is relatively fragmented into separate pairs or small components. Later days more frequently contain links that connect these groups.
04Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time? Several relationships repeatedly appear across the 60 days, while others are temporary and disappear after only a few periods. Some recurring relationships also accumulate substantial transaction value. Delta Components–Fusion Electronics appears especially strong, with about $440K in total transaction value. Ion Systems–Lumen Distribution and Apex Textiles–BlueRiver Logistics also show substantial repeated commercial activity.
05Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time? The network becomes more cross-regional over time. Cross-region links account for about 55% of daily links during days 1–15, increasing to about 72% during days 46–60. This shows that companies in Asia, Europe, and North America become increasingly connected through cross-regional commercial relationships.
