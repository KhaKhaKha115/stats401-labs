TEMPORAL NETWORK QUESTIONS

What changes over time?

QuestionAnswer
01How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days? The network becomes more connected over time. On Day 1, only 6 companies are active with 3 links between them. By Day 60, all 12 companies are active with 9 links connecting them.
02Which companies become more central or commercially active over time? Fusion Electronics, Ion Systems, and Cedar Retail become more active over time. They develop more connections with companies in other regions, and their circles become larger as their transaction volumes increase.
03Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters? Yes: days 1, 7, 11, 13, 17, and 19 contain only three links among six active companies. During these periods, the network is relatively fragmented into separate pairs or small components. Later days more frequently contain links that connect these groups.
04Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time? Some relationships, such as Delta Components and Fusion Electronics, remain active throughout the 60 days. New connections appear after Day 20, while others disappear and reappear on different days. By the final 20 days, Cedar Retail and Ion Systems develop more connections with companies in other regions.
05Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time? The network becomes more cross-regional over time. In the early days, companies mainly trade within their own regions or through a few connections with other regions. By Day 60, more links connect companies across Asia, Europe, and North America.
