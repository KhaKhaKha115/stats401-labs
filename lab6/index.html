Lab 6: Hierarchical Data Visualization: Trees and Treemaps

Design Description

1. Why you chose those visual channels?

I use both rectangle size and numerical labels to encode GDP amount, while color encodes GDP status. GDP amount and GDP status are the two main attributes users need to understand from the visualization, so I chose visually effective channels that allow users to identify them quickly. Rectangle area provides an immediate comparison of GDP size, while the displayed GDP values support more precise comparisons. Color makes the three GDP statuses (Increase, Unchanged, and Decrease) easy to distinguish across countries.

2. How the two segmentation strategies change the appearance/readability of the hierarchy?

The two segmentation strategies organize the same hierarchy differently. Squarify produces more compact and relatively square rectangles, which makes country GDP sizes easier to compare. In this view, large continents such as North America and Asia occupy broad regions, while smaller continents such as Europe, South America, and Oceania are arranged in smaller regions around them. Binary subdivision creates stronger horizontal and vertical partitions. This makes the separation between continent and area groups more apparent, but some smaller countries become narrower rectangles, which can make labels harder to read.
